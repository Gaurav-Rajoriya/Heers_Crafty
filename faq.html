FAQ

Questions, answered.

How can I enquire about a product?

Open any product page and press “Enquire About This Product”. The enquiry form opens with that product already selected, so you only need to add your details and message.

Can I enquire about products from different categories?

Yes. Mention the other pieces in your message, or send a separate enquiry for each – whichever is easier for you.

How can I find a particular product?

Use the Collections menu to open Bedsheets, Clothing, Candles or Home Décor, or choose View All Products to see everything on one page.

Can I request more information about a product?

Of course. Use the enquiry form to ask about anything you’d like to know about a piece, such as colours or sizes, and we’ll reply.

How do I submit an enquiry?

Go to the Contact page, fill in your name, email, phone number, city, product category and message, then press Submit Enquiry. You’ll see a confirmation page once it has been sent.

How will I receive a response?

Our team will reply using the email address or phone number you provide in the form.

Still have a question?

Send us a message and we’ll be happy to help.
